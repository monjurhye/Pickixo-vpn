Subject: Permission request: free Android client app using VPN Gate public servers

Dear VPN Gate team,

I am an independent developer from Bangladesh. I have built a free Android app, "Pickixo VPN"
(package com.pickixo.vpn), that lets users connect to VPN Gate public relay servers over MS-SSTP,
using the server list at http://www.vpngate.net/api/iphone/ and the public "vpn"/"vpn" credentials.

Before I publish it on Google Play, I would like to ask for your permission and follow any rules you have.

How the app works:
- It downloads the server list at most every 30 minutes and caches it on the phone.
- Users choose a country, and the app connects to the highest-scored servers for that country.
- It credits VPN Gate and the University of Tsukuba in the app and on the store listing, and links to vpngate.net.
- Before first use, it clearly tells users that servers are volunteer-run, and that VPN Gate keeps connection
  logs as described in your anti-abuse policy, and asks for their agreement.
- The app is free. It shows Google AdMob ads inside the app to cover development costs. The VPN traffic itself
  is not modified, and the app's own ad traffic does not go through the VPN.

My questions:
1. Do you allow a third-party app like this (free, with in-app ads) to use VPN Gate public servers?
2. Are there any conditions I should follow, such as attribution wording, request-rate limits for the API,
   or a required notice to users?
3. Is there a preferred or HTTPS URL for the server list?

I am happy to change anything you ask, or not to publish if you prefer.

Thank you for running VPN Gate.
