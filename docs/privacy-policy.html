Pickixo VPN Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026 · App: Pickixo VPN (com.pickixo.vpn) · Developer: Pickixo · pickixo.com

In short: Pickixo VPN routes your internet traffic through free public VPN servers run by volunteers of the VPN Gate Academic Experiment Project. Pickixo does not collect, store, share or sell your browsing activity. VPN Gate keeps its own connection logs, described below. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data.

1. What the VPN does

When you tap Connect, Pickixo VPN uses Android's VpnService to create an encrypted tunnel (SSTP over TLS) from your phone to a VPN Gate server. All IPv4 traffic from your other apps goes through that server, so websites see the server's IP address and country instead of yours. IPv6 traffic is blocked while connected to prevent leaks. The Pickixo app's own traffic (ads and the server list) is excluded from the tunnel and uses your normal connection.

2. Data Pickixo itself handles

DataWhere it staysWhy
Your chosen location and app settingsOnly on your phoneTo remember your choice
The public VPN Gate server listOnly on your phone (cache)To show countries and connect
Your internet trafficPasses through the tunnel; never stored or inspected by PickixoTo provide the VPN

Pickixo has no user accounts and runs no servers of its own. We do not log the websites you visit, your IP address, or your connection times.

3. VPN Gate (third-party servers)

The VPN servers are operated by volunteers of the VPN Gate Academic Experiment Project (University of Tsukuba, Japan). Pickixo is not affiliated with VPN Gate. According to VPN Gate's anti-abuse policy:

  • Connection logs are kept for at least 3 months. They include your IP address, connection date and time, the server used, the amount of data, and the destination hosts, IP addresses and ports you connect to.
  • Each volunteer server keeps packet header logs for at least 2 weeks.
  • Logs may be disclosed to police, prosecutors or courts where the law allows.
  • Volunteer operators can see traffic that a website does not encrypt itself (non-HTTPS). Do not use Pickixo VPN for banking or other sensitive accounts.

The app shows this information and asks for your agreement before the VPN can be used.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Pickixo VPN is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention: your IP address (used to estimate general location), product interactions (such as app launches and taps), diagnostic information, and device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID). This data is encrypted in transit. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from the menu → "Ad privacy settings". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

5. Permissions

  • VPN (system dialog): needed to create the VPN tunnel.
  • Internet / network state: needed to connect.
  • Notifications (optional): shows that the VPN is on and a Disconnect button.

6. Children

Pickixo VPN is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country) and is intended for adults.

7. Security

Traffic between your phone and the VPN Gate server is encrypted with TLS. VPN Gate servers use self-signed certificates, so the app cannot verify a server's identity the way a website certificate is verified. Websites that use HTTPS remain encrypted end-to-end.

8. Your choices and deletion

Disconnect at any time from the app or the notification. Uninstalling the app deletes everything Pickixo stored on your phone. For data held by Google, use your Google account and ad settings. For VPN Gate logs, contact the VPN Gate project.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change, and important changes will be shown in the app.
